
Title: Deep Learning for Medical Image Analysis: A Comprehensive Survey
Authors: Sarah Jenkins, Alexander Vance, and Elena Rostova
Abstract: This paper presents a detailed survey of deep learning architectures applied to medical image segmentation, classification, and anomaly detection. We evaluate convolutional networks, vision transformers, and hybrid models across benchmark datasets.
Keywords: Deep Learning, Medical Imaging, Neural Networks, Computer Vision, Artificial Intelligence
Published Date: 2024
Publisher: IEEE Transactions on Medical Imaging
Journal: IEEE Transactions on Medical Imaging
DOI: 10.1109/TMI.2024.1048291
